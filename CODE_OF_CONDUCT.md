# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the Cippus community to be welcoming, inclusive, and free from harassment. Contributors and maintainers are expected to treat each other with respect, empathy, and professionalism.

## Expected behavior

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- respecting different experiences, viewpoints, and good-faith disagreement;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them; and
- focusing on what benefits the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment; personal, political, or derogatory attacks; sexualized language or imagery; doxxing or sharing private information without permission; and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing Cippus publicly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that conflict with this policy, and may issue a private correction or warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant according to the seriousness and pattern of the behavior.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to ptamayocode@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of reporters will be respected.
